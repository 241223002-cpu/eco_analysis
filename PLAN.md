# Project Plan: Green Tech Innovation & Subsidies in Kazakhstani Firms

This document outlines the phases and specific tasks required to complete the research project.

## Phase 1: Project Initiation & Literature Review
- [ ] Define precise research questions and hypotheses.
- [ ] Conduct a comprehensive literature review focusing on:
  - Green technology innovation in emerging and transition economies.
  - The role of government subsidies in environmental and economic performance.
  - Corporate sustainability (ESG) metrics in Kazakhstan and Central Asia.
- [ ] Finalize the theoretical and conceptual framework.
- [ ] Identify key variables (dependent, independent, control, mediating/moderating).

## Phase 2: Data Collection & Preparation
- [ ] Identify and request access to data sources:
  - Bureau of National Statistics of the Agency for Strategic Planning and Reforms of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
  - Firm annual reports and sustainability reports.
  - Financial databases (e.g., Bloomberg, Thomson Reuters, or local databases).
- [ ] Compile data on:
  - Sustainability performance indicators (emissions, waste management, energy efficiency).
  - R&D expenditure on green/clean technologies.
  - Receipt and volume of government environmental subsidies.
  - Control variables (firm size, age, profitability, industry sector, location).
- [ ] Clean and preprocess the panel data.
- [ ] Handle missing values, outliers, and ensure data consistency.
- [ ] Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) and generate descriptive statistics.

## Phase 3: Methodology & Empirical Analysis
- [ ] Select appropriate econometric models:
  - Panel data regression models (Fixed Effects / Random Effects).
  - Consider methods to address endogeneity (e.g., Instrumental Variables, GMM, or Propensity Score Matching).
- [ ] Run preliminary regression models.
- [ ] Conduct robustness checks and sensitivity analysis.
- [ ] Test for multicollinearity and heteroskedasticity.
- [ ] Refine models based on initial findings and statistical tests.
- [ ] Generate final formatted tables and visualizations (e.g., coefficient plots, interaction effect graphs) for the manuscript.

## Phase 4: Drafting the Manuscript
- [ ] Outline the comprehensive structure of the paper.
- [ ] Write the **Introduction**: Context, problem statement, research gap, and contributions.
- [ ] Write the **Literature Review & Hypotheses Development**.
- [ ] Write the **Methodology and Data** section: Detail the sample, variables, and empirical models.
- [ ] Write the **Results** section: Present findings objectively with references to tables/figures.
- [ ] Write the **Discussion**: Interpret findings, compare with prior literature, and outline policy and managerial implications.
- [ ] Write the **Conclusion**: Summary, limitations, and future research directions.
- [ ] Draft the **Abstract** and compile references.

## Phase 5: Review, Finalization, & Submission
- [ ] Internal peer review and feedback integration (from advisors or co-authors).
- [ ] Professional proofreading and language editing.
- [ ] Formatting the manuscript according to the target journal's guidelines.
- [ ] Final check of citations and bibliography.
- [ ] Prepare cover letter and submit the manuscript to the selected journal.

## Ongoing Tasks
- [ ] Regular meetings and progress updates.
- [ ] Version control of code and documents (Git/GitHub).
- [ ] Maintain backups of data and repository.
- [ ] Document all code, data transformations, and analytical decisions.
